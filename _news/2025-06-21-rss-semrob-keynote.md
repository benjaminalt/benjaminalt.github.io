I gave a keynote at the [2nd Workshop on Semantic Reasoning and Goal Understanding in Robotics (SemRob)](https://semrob.github.io/) at the [Robotics: Science and Systems](https://roboticsconference.org/) conference in Los Angeles.

The talk argues that semantic digital twins give robots a representation of their environment that supports both robust low-level control and flexible high-level reasoning, and that this combination is what lets robot behavior generalize to environments the robot was not programmed for.
