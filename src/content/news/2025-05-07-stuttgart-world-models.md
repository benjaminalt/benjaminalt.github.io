---
title: "Invited Talk: World Models 2.0 - From Stochastic Parrots to Intelligent Digital Twins"
date: 2025-05-07 01:00:00-0000
---

I spoke at the Symposium Artificial Intelligence at the [Stuttgart University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hft-stuttgart.de/) on world models for embodied AI.

The talk contrasts the implicit world models learned by large generative models with explicit, queryable digital twins, and argues that robots acting in the physical world need representations that can be inspected and tested.
