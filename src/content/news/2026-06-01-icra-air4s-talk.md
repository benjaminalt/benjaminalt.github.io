date: 2026-06-01 01:00:00-0000
---

I gave an invited talk at the [3rd Workshop on Accelerating Discovery in Natural Science Laboratories with AI and Robotics](https://sites.google.com/view/robotic-accelerated-discovery/) at [ICRA 2026](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/) in Vienna, Austria.

The talk covers how semantic digital twins and cloud-hosted virtual robot labs can make robot-driven experiments reproducible and accessible to researchers without their own robot hardware. It builds on our work on the [AICOR Virtual Research Building](https://vrb.ease-crc.org/) and the RobAuditor execution tracing framework, which won the Best Poster Award at the [previous edition of the workshop](/news/robots-scientific-discovery/) at IROS 2025.
